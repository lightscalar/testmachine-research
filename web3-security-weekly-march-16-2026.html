2 / Emerging Threat: ZK Proof Vulnerabilities

"Default settings ship faster than trust." Two protocols fell victim to incomplete ZK setup ceremonies, representing the first confirmed live exploits of ZK cryptography - not from sophisticated math attacks, but from skipped setup procedures.

FoomCash suffered a $2.26M exploit due to a skipped CLI setup step that left the ZK verifier broken from day one. The attacker scaled up techniques from Veil Cash post-mortem. $1.84M was rescued by Decurity, with $320K kept under protocol bounty, resulting in a net loss of $420K.

This pattern reveals that sophisticated ZK mathematics isn't the vulnerability - incomplete operational procedures are. Multiple protocols are shipping with unfinished trusted setup ceremonies, creating exploitable gaps.
