Paul's "Small Auditors" Concern — A Deep Dive

Paul's skepticism about the small auditor segment is well-founded. Analysis shows:

  • Low ACV: Target ACVs of $30-60K vs. $200K+ for exchanges
  • High CAC: Smaller firms require more hand-holding, increasing acquisition costs
  • Market Saturation: 200+ small audit firms, most struggling with pricing pressure
  • Technology Adoption: Conservative buyers, slow to adopt new tools
  • Churn Risk: Higher volatility, seasonal revenue patterns
Paul's Point: Instead of building a platform for 50 small auditors at $30K each ($1.5M), why not focus on 3 exchanges at $300K each ($900K) with higher probability and faster closes?

Segment Strategy Recommendations

Immediate Focus (Next 6 Months)

  2. Coinbase Expansion: Additional chains, new product modules, increase ACV to $300K+
  3. RWA/Tokenization Pilot: One major client to validate product-market fit

Pause or Pivot

  1. Small Auditors: Pause platform development, focus on 2-3 strategic partnerships instead of 50 small clients

Long-term Options
