07 Board Communication Strategy

Transform board relationship from defensive updates to strategic partnership. Show execution discipline and transparent progress tracking.

Monthly Board Update Format

  1. Pipeline Status: Named accounts, probabilities, movement since last update
  2. Revenue Performance: Actuals vs. projections, leading indicators
  3. Strategic Progress: Specific milestones achieved, blockers encountered
  4. Resource Allocation: How team time and money are being spent
  5. Forward Look: Next month priorities and success criteria
Transparency and Accountability
Ongoing

Principle: Bad news early, good news with evidence, progress with metrics

Frequency: Monthly written updates, quarterly calls, immediate escalation for major issues

Format: Executive summary + detailed appendices for those who want depth

Success Metrics for Board

  • Revenue Growth: Month-over-month MRR increase
  • Pipeline Health: Coverage ratio, conversion rates by stage
  • Customer Satisfaction: NPS scores, renewal rates, expansion revenue
  • Operational Efficiency: Customer acquisition cost, sales cycle length
  • Strategic Progress: Market expansion, product development milestones
