Why These Markets, Not Traditional Audits

  • DEXes don't care about dumping rug pulls on their customers. That's the unfortunate reality.
  • CertiK dominance — you won't unseat them with better marketing in their market.

  • Design partners ≠ revenue. That's the same trap as the last two years.

Dashboard Pivot: Agent Reputation Monitor

The "FICO score for AI agents handling crypto." Every AI agent platform needs this before they handle real money.

  • Agent transaction history analysis — behavioral patterns, risk signals
  • Risk scoring for agent-to-agent interactions
  • Reputation tracking across protocols
  • Security alerts for suspicious agent behavior
  • Insurance integration for agent-managed funds

This positions TestMachine at the intersection of the two hottest trends in crypto: AI and security. It's not evangelizing a new category — it's riding a wave that's already building.
