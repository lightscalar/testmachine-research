Reality Check

Most arbitrage opportunities yield 0.1–0.5% after costs. Infrastructure runs $2K–15K/month. 80%+ of retail attempts fail. This is a professional quantitative finance operation, not a weekend project. Your physics/RL/security background helps, but the competitive landscape demands substantial capital and full-time dedication.

01 Arbitrage Opportunities — The Landscape

DEX-to-DEX Arbitrage (Same Chain)

Price discrepancies between Uniswap v2/v3, SushiSwap, Curve, Balancer on the same chain. Concentrated liquidity vs. constant product inefficiencies. Token launch windows.

  • Net margins: 0.05–0.3% (pre-gas can be 0.2–1.5%, but mainnet gas destroys small trades)
  • Capital: $25K minimum, $100K+ competitive. Flash loans give 10–50x leverage.
  • Competition: EXTREME — dominated by well-funded MEV bots, opportunities last 1–3 blocks
  • L2 opportunity: Polygon, Arbitrum, Optimism have 0.1–0.5% net margins with much lower gas

Cross-Chain Arbitrage

ETH Mainnet ↔ Polygon ↔ Arbitrum ↔ Optimism ↔ Solana. Bridge delays create temporary inefficiencies.

  • Net margins: 0.2–2% per cycle (higher due to friction)
  • Capital: $50K minimum (need liquidity on multiple chains), $200K+ competitive. Cannot use flash loans cross-chain.
  • Competition: HIGH but less saturated — technical barriers limit participants
  • Key challenge: Bridge fees (0.05–0.3%), time delays (2–30 min), multi-chain infrastructure

CEX-to-DEX Arbitrage

Binance/Coinbase vs. Uniswap/SushiSwap. Futures vs. spot. Funding rate arbitrage.

  • Net margins: 0.1–0.8%, higher during volatility spikes
  • Capital: $100K minimum (need presence on both sides), $500K+ competitive
  • Competition: MODERATE-HIGH — limited by CEX API rate limits and KYC
  • Advantage: Lower technical difficulty (6/10), standard REST APIs

Statistical Arbitrage

Mean reversion, pairs trading (ETH/BTC), stablecoin depegs, volatility arbitrage. This is where ML/RL expertise provides the biggest edge.

  • Net margins: 0.1–1% per trade, longer holding periods
  • Capital: $50K minimum, $250K+ competitive
  • Competition: MODERATE — requires quant expertise, less crowded
  • Key advantage: Sustainable edge through ML, higher risk-adjusted returns

MEV Opportunities

Sandwich attacks, liquidation sniping, backrunning, JIT liquidity provision.

  • Net margins: 0.5–10% per successful extraction (high variance)
  • Capital: $100K minimum, $500K+ competitive
  • Competition: EXTREME — professional teams with $10M+ capital
Ethical & Reputation Risk

Sandwich attacks harm retail traders. For a Web3 security company, this carries significant reputation risk. Stick to ethical MEV: backrunning, liquidation sniping, JIT liquidity. Avoid front-running and sandwich attacks.

Reinforcement Learning for Trading

Your Biggest Edge

With deep RL expertise, this is where you differentiate. 10–25% improvement in risk-adjusted returns is achievable with proper implementation.

Applications

  • Optimal execution: TWAP/VWAP splitting, market impact minimization, dynamic slippage
  • Position sizing: Kelly criterion optimization, correlation-aware limits, dynamic leverage
  • Timing: Entry/exit for stat arb, regime identification, volatility prediction

Models That Work

  • PPO — robust for continuous action spaces (position sizing)
  • DQN — discrete actions (buy/sell/hold decisions)
  • Actor-Critic — balancing exploration/exploitation in live markets
  • Transformers — sequence modeling for multi-asset temporal patterns

Feature Engineering (More Important Than Model Choice)

  • Technical: RSI, MACD, Bollinger bands
  • Order book: Bid-ask spread, depth imbalance
  • Cross-exchange: Basis, correlation breakdowns
  • On-chain: Active addresses, tx volume, TVL changes, gas prices, mempool
  • Sentiment: Social volume, news sentiment scores
  • Alternative: GitHub commit activity, Google Trends, whale wallet tracking, bridge flows

Backtesting — Getting It Right

Common Pitfalls That Kill Strategies
  • Lookahead bias in feature construction
  • Survivorship bias in asset selection
  • Transaction cost underestimation — especially gas
  • Slippage modeling — most backtests assume zero impact
  • Overfitting to historical regimes

Validation methodology: Walk-forward analysis (mandatory), cross-validation with time gaps, out-of-sample testing (20–30%), Monte Carlo simulation, then paper trading before live.

Common Failure Modes

Why Most People Lose Money
  • Technical: Infrastructure outages during opportunities, smart contract bugs, API rate limiting during high volume, network congestion
  • Strategic: Overfitting to historical data, inadequate risk management, competition from better-funded operators, market regime changes
  • Operational: Key management errors, regulatory surprises, team burnout, insufficient capital for drawdowns

Regulatory Landscape

  • MEV: Legal gray area, increasing scrutiny
  • Front-running: Potentially illegal in some jurisdictions
  • Tax: Potentially mark-to-market accounting, ordinary income rates (up to 37% US), Section 475 trader status
  • KYC/AML: Required for all CEX interactions

06 Getting Started — Practical Roadmap

Phase 1 · Months 1–2

Paper Trading & Backtesting Setup

Development environment, historical data collection (1–2 years), simple strategy implementation, basic risk management framework. Study Freqtrade, Hummingbot, CCXT.

Budget: $500–2,000
Phase 2 · Months 3–4

Testnet Deployment

Smart contracts on Goerli/Sepolia, flash loan integration, multi-DEX routing, gas optimization, error recovery. Study existing repos: flashloan-arbitrage, mev-template.

Budget: $1,000–3,000
Phase 3 · Months 5–8

Small-Capital Live Trading ($1K–5K)

Mainnet deployment, monitoring dashboards, risk management (max 2% per trade), L2 focus for lower gas. Track Sharpe ratio, max drawdown, cost analysis.

Budget: $5,000–15,000
Phase 4 · Months 9–18

ML/RL Integration

Feature engineering pipeline, model ensemble training, RL for execution timing, sentiment integration, A/B testing framework. GPU infrastructure for training.

Budget: $10,000–30,000
Phase 5 · Months 18–24+

Multi-Strategy Portfolio

Strategy diversification, multi-region deployment, professional monitoring, regulatory compliance, potential team expansion. Institutional-grade operations.

Budget: $50,000–200,000+
