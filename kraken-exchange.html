01 Origin Story

Legal name: Payward, Inc. Founded July 28, 2011 in San Francisco, California by Jesse Powell, who now serves as chairman. Since October 2024, Kraken has been led by co-CEOs Arjun Sethi and Dave Ripley, with approximately 2,200 employees.

Kraken's origin is inseparable from the collapse of Mt. Gox. In 2011, Jesse Powell traveled to Tokyo to consult for Mt. Gox after a major security breach. What he found was deeply alarming—systemic security failures, poor operational practices, and fundamental architectural weaknesses. Rather than trying to fix a broken system, Powell returned to San Francisco and began building a security-first alternative.

The Mt. Gox Connection

When Mt. Gox finally collapsed in February 2014—losing 850,000 bitcoins worth approximately $450 million at the time—Kraken's user base grew 50% within two months. The exchange that was built because of Mt. Gox's failures directly benefited from its demise. Kraken was subsequently chosen by the Mt. Gox bankruptcy trustees to help liquidate and recover lost bitcoins, a testament to the trust the industry placed in Powell's security-first approach.

This founding story established Kraken's core identity: security above all else. Fourteen years later, Kraken has never been hacked—a remarkable track record in an industry plagued by exchange breaches.

02 Product Portfolio (2026)

Kraken has evolved far beyond its origins as a pure crypto exchange. By 2026, it operates a multi-asset financial platform spanning six distinct product lines.

Crypto Exchange

The core business: a spot trading platform supporting 560 cryptocurrencies with deep liquidity, advanced order types, and institutional-grade matching engines. Kraken remains one of the most respected and longest-running crypto exchanges globally.

Stocks, ETFs & Futures

  • Stocks & ETFs: 11,000+ equities available in select US states, commission-free via AlpacaDB integration
  • Futures: Retail futures trading via the NinjaTrader acquisition ($1.5B, May 2025)—Kraken's largest deal ever, giving them a regulated futures brokerage with an established user base

Tokenized Equities

Live since June 2025 for non-US customers. Tokenized versions of Apple, Tesla, Nvidia, and other major equities—allowing 24/7 trading of fractional stock ownership on-chain. This positions Kraken at the intersection of crypto and traditional finance.

Crypto Derivatives

Regulated in Europe via a Cyprus license obtained in May 2025. This builds on their 2019 acquisition of Crypto Facilities (UK-based derivatives platform) and expands Kraken's derivatives offering under a compliant European regulatory framework.

Staking & Wallet

  • Staking: Re-launched in select US states after the 2023 SEC settlement. Careful, compliance-first rollout
  • Kraken Wallet: Multi-chain self-custody wallet supporting 8 blockchains

Institutional & Embed

  • Kraken Institutional: Dedicated division launched March 2024, providing OTC trading, custody, and bespoke services for institutional clients
  • Embed: White-label trading platform for financial institutions. Bunq (European neobank) was the first customer, going live in April 2025. This is Kraken's B2B infrastructure play—enabling banks and fintechs to offer crypto trading under their own brand
Platform Complexity

Kraken now operates 6+ distinct product lines across multiple regulatory jurisdictions, each with different compliance requirements, user bases, and technology stacks. This multi-platform complexity creates significant testing and security challenges—and significant opportunity for TestMachine.

Kraken is one of the most financially successful crypto companies in the world. Revenue doubled year-over-year to $1.5 billion in 2024, with adjusted earnings of $380 million—demonstrating strong profitability in an industry where most exchanges operate at a loss or thin margins.

IPO Plans

Kraken has signaled plans for an IPO in early 2026, which would make it one of the first major crypto exchanges to go public in the US since Coinbase's 2021 direct listing. The IPO will subject Kraken to SOX compliance requirements, public financial reporting, and heightened security scrutiny.

Banking Charter

In 2020, Kraken became the first crypto company to receive a US bank charter—a Wyoming Special Purpose Depository Institution (SPDI). This gives Kraken unique regulatory standing and the ability to operate as a bank for digital assets.

Financial Strength

$1.5B revenue, $380M earnings, $42.8B in custodied assets, and 15M users make Kraken one of the most financially robust potential enterprise customers in the crypto space. Their pre-IPO status creates urgency for security hardening and compliance readiness.

06 Security Posture

Security is Kraken's founding story and core brand promise. The company's security track record is arguably the strongest in the crypto exchange industry.

  • Never hacked in 14+ years of operation—a remarkable record given that exchanges like Mt. Gox, Bitfinex, KuCoin, FTX, and many others have suffered major breaches
  • Security-first founding: Built explicitly because Jesse Powell witnessed Mt. Gox's security failures firsthand
  • Mt. Gox trustee selection: Chosen by bankruptcy trustees to help recover lost bitcoins—a unique endorsement of Kraken's security and operational integrity
  • Wyoming bank charter compliance: Subject to banking-grade security and operational requirements as a Special Purpose Depository Institution
  • Proof-of-reserves audits: Regular cryptographic attestations of asset holdings, providing transparency on solvency
Security as Brand

Unlike most exchanges that treat security as a cost center, Kraken treats it as a core differentiator. Their 14-year unblemished record is a competitive moat that would be catastrophic to lose—especially heading into an IPO where any breach would be devastating to the stock price and public trust.
